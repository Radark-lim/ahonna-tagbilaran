About AhonNa

Developed for Tagbilaran City, Bohol

AhonNa is a web-based evacuation and routing system designed to guide residents to the nearest safe evacuation centers during natural disasters or emergencies. It tracks real-time center capacities, addresses, and status to prevent overcrowding and save lives.
